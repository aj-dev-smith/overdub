Every instrument and effect that ships with Overdub.

Take one home

Use in a new song opens the studio with that device on a track and a few bars to hear it with.

Bring your own

A device you or your agent wrote in a song can be exported from its info card in the rack (a .overdub-device.json) and imported into another song from the Song menu. It is checked again on the way in, and refused with the report if it fails.

Turn the knobs

The faces work. Change a setting and press ▶ again to hear it; an effect's footswitch plays the dry signal.
