Hearing one runs nothing

Each clip is a recording, made when the device was rendered for the shelf, and played here at half volume. It’s what it played then, not a promise of what it plays now.

Trying one asks first

Open in the studio puts a demo song in front of you with the device ready to try. A device is code someone else wrote: the studio asks before it runs any, checks it, and only then puts it on a track, as one step you can undo.

Free, for good

Nothing on the shelf is sold. Each device carries its licence, and names the person who asked for it and the agent that wrote it.
