Who played what.

Takes are signed the way a session is: the player's pencil on the track sheet, the machine's stamp on the box. Nothing gets a coloured bar. Warm is still a person and cool is still an agent; the colour lives in the signature and in the notes.

you
A person: grease pencil. Your name in a china-marker hand, warm, a little loose, with a stroke under it. Your notes get a warm outline.
Claude
An agent: a rubber stamp. Printed capitals in a ruled box, cool, a degree off square. Its notes get a cool outline. Played versus exact, as in the Weave.
house
The house: printed. Demo parts are the form itself. No mark, no colour.
Selected: crop marks. Four corners outside the clip, in cream. No glow, no fill change.
Muted: hatched, colour off. The clip keeps its shape and loses its ink. Every clip can be muted, every track has M, and All off stops every sound at once.
Loop and pointer: yellow grease pencil. The engineer's marks on the tape: the loop bracket, and a ring round what the agent is pointing at.
Leader green: the playhead and the one primary action in a region. Nothing else.
